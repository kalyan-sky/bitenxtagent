# Cart and checkout

## What is in my cart
Your cart holds the items you are about to order, the patient and doctor for the case, the uploaded case files and any coupon. Ask this chat "what's in my cart?" to see the items, total, patient, doctor, coupon and scan upload status.

## Adding the patient and doctor to an order
Each order is for one patient. In the cart, select an existing patient or add a new one, and enter the doctor's name. Both are saved with the order, so you can find the patient's orders later.

## Emptying the cart
You can remove single items from the cart, or clear the whole cart and start again. Cleared items are not ordered.

## Using a coupon
Active coupons can be entered in the cart before checkout. Ask this chat "any coupons for me?" to see the coupons you can use now, with their discount and end date. Only codes shown as active can be applied.

## Checkout
Checkout is the last step: review the items, patient, doctor, files and total, then place the order. If checkout does not complete, check that every item has its required options and uploaded files, then try again or ask this chat to connect you with the team.
