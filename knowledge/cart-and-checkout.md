# Cart and checkout

## What is in my cart
Your cart holds the services you are about to order for the patient, with the doctor details, shipping address, any coupon and the price summary (subtotal, discount, taxes and grand total). Ask this chat "what's in my cart?" to see the items, total, patient, doctor, coupon and scan upload status.

## Adding the patient and doctor to an order
The patient is chosen first, in **Place Order** (Select Patient). The doctor is entered in the cart under **Doctor Details**: type the doctor's name and click Save. Both are saved with the order, so you can find the patient's orders later.

## Shipping address
In the cart, confirm the **Shipping Address** before clicking Place Order. It is required. To change the address on an order that is already placed, ask this chat to connect you with the support team.

## Editing or removing a cart item
Each service in the cart can be edited, viewed or removed using the icons next to it. Use **Add More** to add another service for the same patient.

## Using a coupon
Enter the code in the **Coupon Code** box in the cart before clicking Place Order; the discount shows in the summary. Ask this chat "any coupons for me?" to see the coupons you can use now, with their discount and end date.

## Checkout and how to pay
In the cart, check the services, doctor details, shipping address and total, then click **Place Order**. You are taken to the Razorpay payment page to pay (for example by UPI, card or netbanking). When the payment completes, the Order Confirmed screen shows your order number. If payment or checkout does not complete, check that every service has its required details and try again, or ask this chat to connect you with the team.
