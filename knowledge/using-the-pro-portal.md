# Using the BiteNXT Pro portal

## How to place an order
1. Sign in to the BiteNXT Pro portal.
2. Browse or search the catalog and choose the product or service you need (for example a crown, aligner or design service), then select its options.
3. Add it to your cart.
4. Select the patient for the case (or add a new patient) and enter the doctor's name.
5. Upload the case files: your intraoral scan files, or link your KIXR scan. Wait until the files show as uploaded and validated.
6. Review your cart, apply a coupon if you have one, and complete checkout.
After checkout you can follow the order's progress under your orders in the portal, or ask this chat for the status.

## What products and services are available
The catalog in the Pro portal lists every product and service you can order, grouped by category. Ask this chat
"what products are available?" to see the categories, or name a product (for example "zirconia crown") to search for it.

## Uploading scans and case files
Case files are uploaded in the cart, on the item they belong to, before checkout. You can upload intraoral scan files
from your computer, or use a KIXR scan, which is checked automatically after upload. If a file is rejected or the
validation does not finish, try uploading it again, or ask this chat to connect you with the team.

## Adding a note or document after ordering
If you need to tell us something after the order is placed, open the order in the Pro portal and add a note,
an extra document, or a follow-up on the item. This chat can show you the follow-ups already recorded on your order.

## Reordering
To order the same items again, open the past order in the Pro portal and use reorder. The items are put back in
your cart, where you can check the patient, files and options before checkout.

## Managing patients
You can add, edit and remove patients in the Pro portal. Each order is linked to one patient, so you can find a
patient's orders later. Ask this chat for "orders for patient <name>" to see them.

## Tracking your order
Each order shows its status in the Pro portal. When an order ships, its carrier and tracking number appear in the
order details. Ask this chat for the status of an order by its number (for example "status of order 671").

## Coupons
Active coupons are shown in the cart and can be applied at checkout.
