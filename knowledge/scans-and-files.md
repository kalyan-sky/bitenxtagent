# Scans and case files

## Uploading scan files
Case files are uploaded in the cart, on the item they belong to, before checkout. Upload your intraoral scan files from your computer for each item that needs them. Ask this chat "is my scan uploaded?" to check the upload status of your cart.

## KIXR scans
If you use a KIXR scanner, you can send the KIXR scan to your cart instead of uploading files yourself. KIXR scans are checked automatically after upload (validation), and the cart shows whether the files passed. If validation fails, upload the scan again or ask this chat to connect you with the team.

## A file was rejected or will not upload
Check that you are uploading the scan for the right item and that the upload finished. Try once more; if it still fails, ask this chat to connect you with the support team and mention the product and file type.

## Adding documents after ordering
Files can also be added after an order is placed: open the order and add a document or a follow-up on the item it belongs to.
