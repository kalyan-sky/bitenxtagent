# Scans and case files

## Uploading scan files
Scan files are uploaded while placing the order, in the **Configuration** step of the service: upload the IOS scan STL files (upper, lower and bite) by dragging them into the upload box or clicking it to choose the files. Ask this chat "is my scan uploaded?" to check the upload status of your cart.

## KIXR scans
If you use a KIXR scanner, you can send the KIXR scan to your cart instead of uploading files yourself. KIXR scans are checked automatically after upload (validation), and the cart shows whether the files passed. If validation fails, upload the scan again or ask this chat to connect you with the team.

## A file was rejected or will not upload
Check that you are uploading STL scan files for the right service and that the upload finished. Try once more; if it still fails, ask this chat to connect you with the support team and mention the service and file type.

## Uploading files after placing an order
Files can also be added after the order is placed: open **My Order**, click the order number, and add the attachment on the service it belongs to. You can add a note there too.
