# Your account

## Signing in
Sign in to BiteNXT Pro with your registered email and password. This chat only works while you are signed in, and only shows your own account's orders, cart and coupons.

## New account approval
New BiteNXT Pro accounts may need to be approved before you can sign in and order. If you registered but cannot sign in yet, ask the support team to check your account.

## Forgotten password
Use the forgotten password option on the BiteNXT Pro sign-in page if it is shown, or ask the support team to help you reset it. Never share your password in this chat.

## Business details
Your account includes your business name, contact phone and customer type. If any of these are wrong, update them in your account details or ask the support team.

## Addresses
Your saved addresses are used for shipping your orders. You can add or update them in your account. To change the address on an order that is already placed, ask this chat to connect you with the support team.

## Account documents
You can attach documents to your account in BiteNXT Pro, for example documents the team needs to approve or set up your account.
