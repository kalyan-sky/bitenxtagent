# Your account

## Signing in
Sign in at pro.bitenxt.com with your registered email and password. This chat only works while you are signed in, and only shows your own account's orders, cart and coupons.

## Creating an account (sign up)
On the sign-in page, click **Sign Up** and choose how you work with us:
- **Clinic/Lab**: enter the business name, your first and last name, email and phone.
- **Private Practitioner**: enter your first and last name, email and contact phone.
Fill in the remaining details on the form and submit it.

## New account approval
New BiteNXT Pro accounts may need to be approved before you can sign in and order. If you registered but cannot sign in yet, ask the support team to check your account.

## Forgotten password
Click **Forget Password?** on the BiteNXT Pro sign-in page to reset it, or ask the support team for help. Never share your password in this chat.

## Business details
Your account includes your business name, contact phone and customer type. Open **My Profile** to check them, or ask the support team if any of these are wrong.

## Addresses
Your saved addresses are used for shipping your orders, and you confirm the shipping address in the cart for each order. To change the address on an order that is already placed, ask this chat to connect you with the support team.

## Account documents
You can attach documents to your account in BiteNXT Pro, for example documents the team needs to approve or set up your account.
