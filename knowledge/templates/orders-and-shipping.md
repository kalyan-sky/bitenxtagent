# Orders and shipping

## Order statuses explained
Pending: we have your order and are checking the case files. Processing: the case is with our design team.
In review: the design is being checked before production. Shipped: your case has left our lab and a tracking
number is available. Complete: the order has been delivered. [Adjust to match your Magento order statuses.]
