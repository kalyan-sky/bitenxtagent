# Patients

## Adding a patient
Click **Add Patients** in **Place Order** (or open **Patients** from the menu), enter the patient's name, age and gender, and click Save. The patient then appears in your list, ready to select for an order.

## Managing patients
Open **Patients** from the menu to see and manage your patients. Each order is for one patient, whom you select in the first step of Place Order.

## Patient privacy in this chat
For privacy, this chat shows patients by first name and last initial only, and never shows other clinics' patients or orders.
