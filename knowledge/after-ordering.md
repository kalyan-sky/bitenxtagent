# After you order

## Tracking an order
Each order shows its status in your order history in BiteNXT Pro. When an order ships, its carrier and tracking number appear in the order details. Ask this chat "status of order <number>" for the status, items, total and tracking, or "my orders" for your latest orders.

## Order statuses
Orders move through statuses while your case is designed, checked and shipped, for example pending, processing and complete. The exact status names are shown on each order. Ask this chat for any order's current status.

## How many orders do I have
Ask this chat "how many orders do I have?" for your total and how many are in each status.

## Adding a note, document or follow-up
If you need to tell us something after ordering, open the order in BiteNXT Pro and add a note, an extra document, or a follow-up (a note with documents) on the item it is about. Ask this chat "follow-ups on order <number>" to see what has been recorded on an order.

## Finding orders for a patient
Your order history can be searched by patient name. Ask this chat "orders for patient <name>" to see that patient's orders.
