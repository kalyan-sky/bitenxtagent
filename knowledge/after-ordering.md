# After you order

## Adding attachments or notes after ordering
You can add attachments and notes to an order after it is placed, for example extra scan files, photos, documents or instructions for the lab.
1. Open **My Order** in BiteNXT Pro.
2. Click the order number to open the order.
3. Each service in the order has its own attachments and notes. Find the service it is about and add your attachment and/or note there.
Add files and notes on the service they belong to, so the team sees them with the right item. Ask this chat "follow-ups on order <number>" to see what has been recorded on an order.

## Tracking an order
Every order is listed under **My Order** with its order number, patient, date, service, status and price. Click the order number to see all its details. Ask this chat "status of order <number>" for the status, items, total and tracking, or "my orders" for your latest orders.

## Order statuses
New orders are reviewed by our team first and show as pending. Once approved they are processed, and finished orders show as completed. The exact status names are shown on each order. Ask this chat for any order's current status.

## How many orders do I have
Ask this chat "how many orders do I have?" for your total and how many are in each status.

## Finding orders for a patient
**My Order** can be searched by patient name. Ask this chat "orders for patient <name>" to see that patient's orders.
