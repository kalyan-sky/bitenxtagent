# Appointments and help

## Booking an appointment or consultation
You can request an appointment with the BiteNXT team from BiteNXT Pro: choose the service, a time that suits you and add a short description. The team receives your request by email and confirms with you.

## What this chat can do
This chat can show your order status and tracking, your latest orders and order counts, follow-ups on an order, your cart and scan upload status, your active coupons, and search the product catalog. It can also answer how-to questions about BiteNXT Pro and pass your request to the support team.

## Talking to a person
Ask this chat to connect you with the support team at any time, for example to change or cancel an order, ask about a remake or billing, or report a problem. The team follows up by email.
