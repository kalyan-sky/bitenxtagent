# Ordering in BiteNXT Pro

## How to place an order
1. Sign in to BiteNXT Pro (pro.bitenxt.com) and open **Place Order** from the menu.
2. **Select Patient**: pick the patient from your list (you can search by name), or click **Add Patients** to add a new patient with their name, age and gender.
3. **Select Service**: pick the dental service for the patient, for example a bridge, veneers, inlays and overlays, pontics, coping/cutback crowns or an acrylic partial denture.
4. **Configuration**: fill in the details for the service. Upload the IOS scan (STL files: upper, lower and bite) by dragging them in or clicking to upload, and add your RX notes. Then click **Add to Cart**.
5. **Cart**: confirm the shipping address, enter the doctor's name under Doctor Details, enter a coupon code if you have one, and click **Place Order**. Use **Add More** to add another service for the same patient first.
6. **Payment**: complete the payment on the Razorpay page.
7. The **Order Confirmed** screen shows your order number. Our team reviews the order; once it is approved you get a confirmation email and the order goes into production.
Your order then appears under **My Order**. Ask this chat "status of order <number>" at any time. If you need to send more files or notes later, you can add them to the order in My Order.

## Ordering for a new patient
In **Place Order**, click **Add Patients**, enter the patient's name, age and gender, and save. The new patient appears in the list; select them to continue to Select Service.

## Choosing a service and its configuration
After selecting the patient, Place Order shows the services you can order, grouped by category (for example Crowns and Bridges). Select one to open its Configuration, where you upload the IOS scan (upper, lower and bite STL files), write RX notes and fill in the options the service needs. The service can only be added to the cart once the required details are filled in.

## What happens after I place an order
After payment, the Order Confirmed screen shows your order number (for example #000000608). The order is then reviewed by our team. Once approved, you get a confirmation email and the order is processed. You can follow it in **My Order**, where each order shows its patient, date, service, status and price.

## What products and services can I order
Everything you can order is shown in **Place Order** after you select a patient, grouped by category. Ask this chat "what products are available?" to see the categories with examples, or name a product (for example "zirconia crown") to search for it.

## Can I change or cancel an order
You can't change or cancel an order from this chat or after checkout yourself. Ask this chat to connect you with the support team and include the order number; the team will reply to your registered email.

## Reordering a previous order
To order the same items again, open the past order in **My Order** and choose reorder if it is offered, or place a new order for the same patient from **Place Order**.
