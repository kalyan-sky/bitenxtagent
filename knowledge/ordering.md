# Ordering in BiteNXT Pro

## How to place an order
1. Sign in to BiteNXT Pro.
2. Find the product or service in the catalog (browse the categories or search by name) and choose its options, such as the teeth it applies to.
3. Add it to your cart.
4. In the cart, select the patient for the case (or add a new patient) and enter the doctor's name.
5. Upload the case files for each item: your intraoral scan files, or a KIXR scan. Wait until the upload shows as complete and validated.
6. Apply a coupon if you have one, review the cart, and complete checkout.
After checkout, the order appears in your order history, where you can follow its status. You can also ask this chat "status of order <number>".

## What products and services can I order
Everything you can order is in the BiteNXT Pro catalog, grouped by category. Ask this chat "what products are available?" to see the categories with examples, or name a product (for example "zirconia crown") to search for it.

## Product options and teeth selection
Many products have options you choose before adding them to the cart, such as dropdowns, checkboxes or text fields for instructions. Some products also ask which teeth they apply to. Fill in every required option; the product cannot be added to the cart until they are complete.

## Can I change or cancel an order
You can't change or cancel an order from this chat or after checkout yourself. Ask this chat to connect you with the support team and include the order number; the team will follow up by email.

## Reordering a previous order
To order the same items again, open the past order in your order history and choose reorder. The items are put back in your cart, where you check the patient, doctor, files and options before checking out again.
